# Analytics and account

## Home analytics

**Home** is the dashboard for owners and managers:

- Today, week, and period numbers
- Charts for volume, status, source, queue, service, wait and serve times, peak hours
- A live tickets card
- Period controls and CSV export when available

Counter staff see a simpler desk Home instead of full charts.

## Account

Open **Account** to:

- Update name and profile image
- View email (read-only)
- Change password
- Review sessions and sign out
- See memberships across businesses

> Tip: export CSV from Home at the end of the week if you share numbers with leadership outside NextUp.
