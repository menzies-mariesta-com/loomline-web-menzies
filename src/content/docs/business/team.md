# Team and invites

Open **Team** in the sidebar.

## Members and invites

- **Members**: people who already have access, with readable roles.
- **Invites**: pending invitations waiting to be accepted.

Click **Invite** (or **Invite teammate**) to send an email invite. The person opens the invite link, sets their password if needed, then joins your business. You can also **Provision** a user directly when that action appears on a pending invite.

Station accounts created from **Devices** show up on Team as well.

> Tip: invite a manager before a busy day so they can help with Devices and Config without sharing the owner login.
