# Devices

Open **Devices** in the sidebar, then click **Pair device**.

## TV

1. Choose type **TV**, give it a name, pick the site, and optionally limit which queues appear.
2. Copy the pairing **Code** from the table, or click **Open TV**.
3. On the TV browser, the board loads. Click the board for fullscreen.
4. Voice announcements follow languages and repeat settings from **Config**.

If you leave queue binding empty, the TV shows all queues for that site. Bound queues filter now serving, upcoming, and missed.

## Kiosk

1. Choose type **Kiosk**, same site, optional queues.
2. Open the kiosk link for that code (from the row actions).
3. Guests tap a service to take a number.

## Counter station

1. Choose type **Agent**.
2. Enter a station email and password (at least 8 characters), and optionally bind a counter.
3. Staff sign in with that account on the counter PC. Stations do not use a public pairing link.

## Codes, revoke, and delete

- **New pairing code** immediately invalidates the old TV or kiosk link.
- **Revoke** makes public links fail until you reactivate.
- **Delete** removes the device for good.

> Tip: keep the TV and kiosk codes handy on the Devices table. Rotate a code if a screen was shared by mistake.
