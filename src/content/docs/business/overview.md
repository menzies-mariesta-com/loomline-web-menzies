# Overview

NextUp helps guests take a number, staff call people to the counter, and waiting-room screens show who is next. You run everything from your business workspace after you sign in.

## Pieces of the floor

| Piece | Who uses it | What it does |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Business workspace | Owners and managers | Set up the floor, team, tickets, and settings |
| Kiosk | Guests | Take a number by tapping a service |
| TV display | Waiting room | Shows now serving, upcoming, and missed numbers |
| Agent desk | Counter staff | Call next, serve, and finish tickets |
| Guest ticket page | One guest | Personal status and QR for that ticket |

## First-day path

1. Sign in and open your business from Workspaces.
2. Create a site, services, a department, open counters, and queues (see Floor setup).
3. Open **Devices** and pair a TV and a kiosk (optional: create a counter station).
4. On the kiosk, take a test number.
5. On **Agent**, click **Call next** and watch the TV update.

The pages that follow walk each step in calm, click-by-click detail.
