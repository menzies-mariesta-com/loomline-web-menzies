# Creating tickets

Tickets are created when someone takes a number or checks in. You can review every ticket under **Tickets** in the sidebar.

## Ways to create a ticket

| Method | Where | Typical use |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Kiosk | Public kiosk screen | Lobby self-service |
| Guest desk | **Guest** in the sidebar | Staff or invited guest take-a-number |
| Agent issue | **Agent** → issue ticket | Walk-ups without a kiosk |
| Appointment check-in | **Appointments** → **Check in** | Booked visits that join the queue |

After a ticket is issued, guests can open their personal ticket page (QR or **View ticket**) to see status.

## Reviewing tickets

On **Tickets**, filter by status and how the ticket was created. Use this list when a guest asks where they stand or when you audit the day.

> Tip: issue one test ticket from the kiosk, then open **Tickets** to confirm it appears before you go live.
