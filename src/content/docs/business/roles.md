# Sign in and roles

Sign in with email and password from the login page. After login you land on Workspaces (or straight into a business if you only have one).

## Who can do what

| Role | What they see |
|------|----------------|
| Owner | Full business workspace: setup, team, config, analytics |
| Manager | Same floor and team tools as the owner for day-to-day running |
| Counter staff | Home and **Agent** only. Setup pages stay closed |
| Guest | Guest take-a-number and appointments when invited |
| Platform admin | Platform tools plus any business (NextUp operators) |

## Counter stations

When you create an **Agent** device under **Devices**, NextUp also creates a station sign-in (email and password). That account is meant for the counter PC. After login, the sidebar only shows **Home** and **Agent**.

## Your account

Use **Account** (from the shell menu) to update your name or photo, change password, review sessions, sign out, and see which businesses you belong to.
