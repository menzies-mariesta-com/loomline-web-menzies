# Agent desk

Open **Agent** in the sidebar (or use counter shortcuts from Home). Station accounts land here after sign-in.

## Daily loop

1. Select the **queue** and **counter** (the counter must be open).
2. Click **Call next** to move a waiting ticket to called. The TV updates and may announce.
3. Click **Serve**, then complete when the guest is done.
4. Use **Recall**, **Hold**, or **No-show** when needed.
5. **Transfer** sends a ticket to another queue. **Redirect** needs destinations set up first.
6. **Issue ticket** covers walk-ups when there is no kiosk nearby.

Station accounts only see **Home** and **Agent**. Setup pages stay unavailable for those logins.

> Tip: keep one queue and one open counter selected before the lobby opens, so the first **Call next** is one click.
