# Sponsors and partners

This page is for organizations that sponsor, co-market, or list NextUp beside their own brand. Keep credit clear and quiet. Soft Wash should feel like a calm lobby, not a loud endorsement banner.

For logo colors and wordmark rules, see [Brand guidelines](/docs/brand/overview). For official URLs and short attribution lines, see [How to reference NextUp](/docs/brand/referencing).

## Credit NextUp clearly

| Do | Prefer |
|----|--------|
| Name | **NextUp** (or **NextUp QMS** once if queue context is unclear) |
| Design language | Soft Wash (visual system only, not a second product) |
| Link target | Official `{ORIGIN}/` or `{ORIGIN}/docs` from your deployment |
| Visual mark | Text wordmark or public favicon / OG assets from Visual assets |

Replace `{ORIGIN}` with the canonical host. Do not point guests at a third-party mirror as if it were the official site.

## Partnership mention rules

- Lead with NextUp for product credit. <a href="https://menzies.mariesta.com/" class="link-inherit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Menzies</a> is optional corporate context only.
- Say what the relationship is in plain words: sponsor, technology partner, or listed integration. Do not invent exclusive or certified seals NextUp has not granted.
- Keep partner chrome quieter than Soft Wash surfaces you embed or screenshot.
- Prefer one calm credit line over a cluster of badges and promo stickers.

Suggested short credit:

> Powered by NextUp. Soft Wash queue experience.

Or:

> Partner: NextUp QMS (`{ORIGIN}`)

## Linking to NextUp

| Intent | Path |
|--------|------|
| Product home | `/` |
| Docs hub | `/docs` |
| Brand guidelines | `/docs/brand/overview` |
| This partners guide | `/docs/partners/overview` |
| Pricing | `/pricing` |

Do not deep-link marketing guests into private console paths (`/admin`, `/b/{id}`, `/workspaces`). Floor URLs with pairing codes are operational, not partner collateral.

## Soft Wash attribution

When you reuse Soft Wash visuals:

- Keep mint wash + ink text; avoid purple-indigo glow themes next to NextUp marks.
- Do not stretch, outline, or recolor the wordmark.
- Credit NextUp when you reuse public OG or favicon artwork.

## Contact and next steps

1. Align naming and URLs with this page and How to reference NextUp.
2. Use Brand guidelines before shipping partner pages or press kits.
3. For commercial partnership terms, reach out through your usual NextUp contact. This docs page is guidance, not a signed agreement.
