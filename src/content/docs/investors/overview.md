# Investor materials

Use this page when NextUp appears in fundraising decks, diligence memos, or portfolio updates. Stay factual. Soft Wash is the product look and feel, not a separate company or financing vehicle.

Related pages: [How to reference NextUp](/docs/brand/referencing), [Brand guidelines](/docs/brand/overview).

## Naming

| Use | When |
|-----|------|
| **NextUp** | Default product name in slides and prose |
| **NextUp QMS** | When you need to stress queue management |
| **Soft Wash** | Design language and UI tone only |
| <a href="https://menzies.mariesta.com/" class="link-inherit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Menzies</a> | Parent context when corporate ownership matters |

Do not invent spellings (NextUp, next-up). Do not present Soft Wash as a standalone financed product.

## Official URLs

Share the deployment `ORIGIN` (no trailing slash), then these paths:

| Intent | Path |
|--------|------|
| Home | `/` |
| Pricing | `/pricing` |
| Docs hub | `/docs` |
| This investors guide | `/docs/investors/overview` |
| Brand guidelines | `/docs/brand/overview` |
| Privacy | `/privacy` |
| Terms | `/terms` |

Never treat private app paths (`/admin`, `/workspaces`, tenant consoles) as public marketing or diligence URLs.

## Soft Wash positioning

- Soft Wash describes calm mint washes, mineral ink, and quiet type on NextUp surfaces.
- In investor copy, lead with NextUp (the product). Mention Soft Wash only when visual brand or UX differentiation is the point.
- Prefer short, concrete floor language (kiosk, ticket, counter) over hype adjectives.

## What not to claim

Do not state or imply:

- Guaranteed growth, revenue, or customer counts NextUp has not published to you.
- That an embed, fork, or white-label is the official NextUp cloud.
- Certification, exclusivity, or regulatory status NextUp has not granted in writing.
- That Soft Wash is a separate legal entity or fundraise target.

If a figure is estimates or internal, label it clearly. Prefer linking to official pages over restating stale metrics.

## Diligence checklist

1. Product name and URLs match this page.
2. Screenshots follow Brand guidelines (no fake badges over Soft Wash UI).
3. Claims about Cloud vs On-premise match current product copy on Pricing and legal pages.
4. Contact your NextUp counterpart for current contract or data-room packs. Docs here are product guidance, not legal advice.
