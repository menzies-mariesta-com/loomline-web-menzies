# Brand guidelines

Use this page when you build partner pages, embeds, emails, or store listings that sit next to NextUp. Soft Wash is the visual system: calm mint washes, mineral ink, and quiet type. It should feel like a lobby that breathes, not a neon dashboard.

Related brand docs:

| Page | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| [How to reference NextUp](/docs/brand/referencing) | Official site paths, naming in prose, attribution lines |
| [Visual assets](/docs/brand/assets) | Favicon, Open Graph, email hero URLs from `ORIGIN` |

## Names

| Use | When |
|-----|------|
| **NextUp** | Default product name in UI, docs, and press |
| **NextUp QMS** | When you need to stress queue management |
| **Soft Wash** | The design language and UI kit tone, not a separate product |

Do not invent spellings (NextUp, next-up, Next Up). Do not call the product Soft Wash alone in customer-facing copy.

## Logo and wordmark

Marketing chrome uses a **text wordmark**: the word NextUp in Fraunces, ink color, tight tracking. There is no separate illustrated mascot required for attribution.

- Prefer the wordmark over a cropped screenshot of the app chrome.
- Keep clear space around the word roughly the height of the capital L.
- Use dark ink on light wash backgrounds, or reverse to white only on deep teal or navy fields.
- Do not outline, bevel, stretch, or put the wordmark inside a busy photo collage.
- Do not recolor the wordmark to arbitrary brand hues (no purple glow, no neon).

Small marks for tabs and emails use the public favicon assets (see Visual assets).

## Soft Wash colors

Core tokens used on the marketing site and transactional email:

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Mint | `#d8eee6` | Soft wash background |
| Mint deep | `#b8ddd0` | Borders, soft fills |
| Ink | `#1c2a28` | Primary text and wordmark |
| Coral | `#e07a5f` | Accent and primary CTA energy |
| Teal | `#3d7a6f` | Links and secondary emphasis |
| Navy | `#2c3e50` | Muted labels and footers |
| Cream | `#f7f4ef` | Warm paper alternate |

Pair mint wash + ink text for calm surfaces. Use coral sparingly for one primary action. Avoid stacking every accent in one viewport.

## Typography

| Role | Family | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| Display / headings | **Fraunces** | Semibold; slight negative letter-spacing |
| Body / UI | **Outfit** | Regular, medium, and semibold |

Fallbacks: Georgia for Fraunces, system UI sans for Outfit. Do not substitute Inter, Roboto, or Arial as the face of NextUp marketing.

## Tone

- Calm, direct, guest-friendly language.
- Prefer short sentences and concrete floor words (kiosk, ticket, counter).
- No em dashes in NextUp UI or published copy; use a period, colon, or hyphen.

## Do and do not

**Do**

- Link to official NextUp URLs for product, pricing, and docs (see How to reference NextUp).
- Keep partner chrome visually quieter than the Soft Wash surfaces you embed.
- Credit NextUp when you reuse public OG or favicon artwork.

**Do not**

- Imply that your fork or white-label is the official NextUp cloud.
- Mix Soft Wash mint with purple-indigo gradient themes or heavy multi-layer glow.
- Place detached promo stickers or fake badges over NextUp screenshots.
- Use NextUp marks to endorse unrelated products.
